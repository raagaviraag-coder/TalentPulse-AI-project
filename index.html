ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why TalentPulse exists

Turnover is expensive and mostly predictable. TalentPulse closes the gap between data and decisions.

AI-Driven Prediction

A tuned Random Forest model trained on historical HR records forecasts attrition risk per employee with 94% accuracy.

Root Cause Analysis

Every prediction is decomposed into the specific factors driving it, so HR knows exactly what to fix.

Proactive Retention

Actionable, prioritized recommendations turn risk scores into real interventions before resignation letters arrive.
